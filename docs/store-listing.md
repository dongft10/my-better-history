## Description —— 中文版（zh-CN，529 字 ✓ ≤1000 字）

```
My Better History —— 一款现代化的浏览器历史记录管理器。它取代浏览器自带的"历史记录"页面，让查找、筛选和管理浏览记录变得轻松高效。

核心功能：

【日历按日浏览】页面左上角常驻日历，点击任意日期即可查看当天的全部访问记录；有记录的日期带有蓝紫色标记，一眼可辨；还支持"前一天/后一天"快捷切换，连续翻看历史非常顺手。

【智能搜索】输入关键字即可从全部历史记录中即时检索，支持标题和网址匹配；更支持拼音搜索——输入"sousuo"也能找到标题含"搜索"的页面，命中的汉字会高亮显示，中英文查找都方便。

【时间筛选】今天、昨天、本周、本月、全部，五种时间范围一键切换；记录按日期倒序分组，每天访问了什么一目了然。

【批量管理】可勾选多条记录或整个日期的记录，一键批量删除；也支持按搜索结果批量清理，历史维护省时省力。

【明暗主题】一键切换浅色与深色主题，适配白天与夜晚的使用环境。

【键盘快捷键】支持方向键导航、回车打开、空格勾选、Delete 删除等快捷键，操作流畅高效。

【多语言】内置中文、英文、法文三种界面语言，自动跟随浏览器语言设置。

隐私与安全：
本扩展完全在本地运行，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据；无需登录，无任何广告，可放心使用。

免费使用，开源发布。
```

## Description —— English（en-US，约 420 词 ✓）

```
My Better History is a modern browsing history manager. It replaces the browser's built-in history page with a clean, fast interface for finding, filtering, and managing your browsing records.

Calendar day view
A calendar stays on the top-left of the page. Click any date to see exactly what you visited that day. Dates with records are marked in blue-purple for quick scanning, and you can step backward or forward one day at a time.

Smart search
Search across all history instantly by title or URL. Pinyin search is also supported — type "sousuo" and pages whose Chinese titles contain "搜索" will be found, with the matched characters highlighted.

Time filters
Today, yesterday, this week, this month, and all — switch with one click. Records are grouped by date in reverse order, so you always know what you visited each day.

Batch management
Select multiple records or whole day groups and delete them in one click. You can also bulk-clear results from a search, keeping your history tidy.

Light & dark themes
Toggle between light and dark themes with one click to match your environment.

Keyboard shortcuts
Navigate with arrow keys, open with Enter, select with Space, and delete with Delete for a smooth experience.

Multi-language
Built-in Chinese, English, and French interfaces that follow your browser language automatically.

Privacy & security
Everything runs locally in your browser. No personal data is collected, transmitted, or sold. No login required, no ads.

Free to use and open source.
```
